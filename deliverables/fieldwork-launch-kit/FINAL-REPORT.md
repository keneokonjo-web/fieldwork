# Fieldwork launch foundation: final report

Branch `claude/exciting-maxwell-l5r45m` of `keneokonjo-web/fieldwork`. Packaged copy: `deliverables/fieldwork-launch-kit/` and `deliverables/fieldwork-launch-kit.zip`. Nothing has been deployed, bought, registered or sent.

## 1. Completed and tested in this environment
- **Identity (Concept 2, The Brief, final direction):** primary wordmark (lowercase Schibsted Grotesk with a halftone-disc dot), dot-matrix disc symbol, the survey-target icon (favicon, app icon, avatar), lockup; light, dark, cobalt and one-colour versions as SVG and PNG; Open Graph image; dot-terrain library; 8 templates (social, story, LinkedIn banner, slides, document cover, email signature); 8 graphic assets; brand guidelines (PDF and editable HTML); design tokens (CSS and doc). Tested: rendered and inspected; a token test ties CSS to the palette; contrast ratios measured.
- **Website:** all 19 routes rebuilt on Concept 2: black top bar as navigation (wordmark, pages, slogan), the concept's hero (corner marks, dot terrain, index of starter tasks, giant cropped wordmark), hairline index lists, Problem / Practice / Result rows, short copy throughout. Free guided exercise, workflow assessment, GBP/NGN pricing toggle, Field Notes (4 articles), legal drafts, custom 404, and 29 licensed stock photographs in hairline cells. Tested: typecheck, lint, 26 unit tests, production build, 18 browser behaviour tests, axe 0 violations, link crawl, overflow checks. Details: `qa/FINAL-QA-REPORT.md`, `qa/DESIGN-REVIEW.md`.
- **Documents written:** brand strategy; design tokens and website design brief (updated to Concept 2); consumer marketing (strategy, 30-day calendar, video kit, social kit, 5 email sequences, pricing assumptions, funnel and metrics, workflow, trackers); corporate sales kit (capability statement, one-pager, 14-slide deck as PPTX, discovery and demo guides, proposal and SOW templates, pricing assumptions, objections, outreach, follow-ups, marketing plan, partnership pack, CRM and pipeline templates); product and operations (PRD for the future onboarding platform, sitemap, architecture, exercise spec, roadmap, SEO keyword map and checklist, analytics spec, deployment guide, domain/DNS/email/social guide, legal review list, launch checklist, repository guide, asset inventory, dependencies, limitations register, CRM definitions, attribution guide, QA re-run guide). PDFs built for the documents that get sent to people. Marketing and sales documents that mention colours and fonts were updated to the new brand; their copy was otherwise not rewritten.

## 2. Prepared but not connected
Lead capture adapters (webhook, Resend, dev file) are coded and the webhook path is tested against a local mock; none is connected to a real destination, so live forms honestly report "not connected". Analytics is coded, consent-gated and off until a domain is configured. Indexing is off until a URL and a flag are set. Payments are not integrated. No email list platform exists.

## 3. Deferred or not possible from here
Deploying to Vercel (no authorisation); buying or configuring a domain or DNS; creating email, social, analytics or payment accounts; legal review of the privacy, terms and cookie pages; trademark clearance for "Fieldwork" (the word is common, with UK market-research users); photographer lookups for the stock images; testing on real devices, other browsers and screen readers; the onboarding platform (PRD only); real photography, real testimonials and case studies (none exist, and none are invented).

## 4. Assumptions and numbers to confirm
Every price, target and rate in the marketing and sales documents is a labelled hypothesis. Regulatory and market statements are marked "verify before use". The domain checks (14 October 2026) reported fieldworklearn.com, fieldworkhq.co.uk and several .study, .training and .org options available and the exact name unavailable; re-check at purchase. See `docs/operations/OUTSTANDING-DEPENDENCIES.md` for the founder decisions that unblock launch.

## 5. Honest caveats
The design is reviewed by the builder only. The deck is functional, not polished. Stock photography is category-appropriate rather than distinctive. The design was reset more than once: the first generic design was rejected, then a blend of concepts 2 and 3 was built, then replaced by Concept 2 alone at the founder's direction. The earlier concept recommendations are superseded (see `01-brand/concepts/recommendation.md`). Old brand assets are not kept in the repository except in git history.
