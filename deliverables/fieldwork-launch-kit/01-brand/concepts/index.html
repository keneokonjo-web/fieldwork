Three directions

Built after inspecting Anwulika's Field Notes, Anveril, Compound, the AI in Design Report, DES, The Lookback and Vide Infra in a real browser. Each board: idea, borrowed lessons, moodboard of original studies, palette, type, wordmark, graphic devices, hero (desktop and mobile), learning card, editorial card, business extension, and an honest evaluation.

All three share one original invention: the field, a generative contour terrain seeded by each exercise or note number. The directions differ in how they express it.
