# Concept decision (supersedes the earlier recommendation)

The first version of this file recommended Concept 1. **That recommendation is withdrawn.** After reviewing the three round-2 concepts the founder rejected Concept 1 and chose a blend:

| Concept | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1. (rejected) | Not used. Kept in this folder only as a record. |
| 2. The Brief | **About 35% of the system.** Kept: the giant wordmark, bracket numerals, the hero composition and large type. Its wordmark is archived at `public/brand/logo/archive/`. |
| 3. Specimen Room | **About 65% of the system.** Kept: the benchmark symbol, the plate, the museum label, the palette. |

The blend is built and live in the repository: logo system (`scripts/brand/build-identity.mjs`), tokens (`src/styles/tokens.css`), website, templates (`brand/templates`) and guidelines (`brand/guidelines`).

Register rule: the consumer side leans Specimen Room (serif headlines, chalk ground, plates); the business side leans The Brief (grotesque display, bracket numerals, ruled grids). Same symbol, plates and labels throughout.
