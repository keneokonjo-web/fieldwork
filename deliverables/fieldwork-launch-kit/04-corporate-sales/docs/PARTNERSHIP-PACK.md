# Partnership pack

Version 0.1, 14 October 2026. Who Fieldwork could partner with, what we offer, a shareable partner brief and referral-terms placeholders. **No partnerships exist today. Do not imply any.** Names below are examples of types and candidates to research, marked verify before use; none has been contacted. Partnerships involving money need written agreement reviewed by a solicitor.

## 1. Why partner

A new company with no case studies borrows trust and reach from established bodies. Partners also reduce sales effort, which the pricing model shows is the largest cost (SALES-PRICING-ASSUMPTIONS.md).

## 2. Who to approach

### 2.1 Property software vendors and user groups
**Why:** their customers use the tools daily; they may value training that makes customers use their systems and content better.
**Candidates to research (verify before use; examples only):** UK estate and lettings CRM and software suppliers (e.g. Reapit, Alto, Street, Jupix, Expert Agent, Rex); property portals' agent communities; Nigerian property listing platforms and software (e.g. PropertyPro.ng, Nigeria Property Centre, Private Property NG).
**Offer:** a free clinic for their user group; co-branded fact-check guide; a referral arrangement.
**Risk:** a vendor may want exclusivity or endorsement claims. Stay tool-agnostic and independent; do not claim vendor partnership unless in writing.

### 2.2 Associations and professional bodies
**Why:** members trust them; they run events and newsletters.
**Candidates (verify before use):** UK: Propertymark (NAEA and ARLA), RICS, NRLA, The Property Ombudsman, Property Redress Scheme, regional property networks, local chambers of commerce. Nigeria: NIESV, ESVARBON, REDAN, state chambers of commerce, Lagos Chamber of Commerce and Industry [all verify].
**Offer:** a member webinar ("Check the draft"), a one-page staff AI-use rule outline, member rate for pilots.
**Risk:** associations are careful about endorsements and regulatory topics. Say plainly it is training, not compliance advice. Expect slow decisions.

### 2.3 Training bodies and providers
**Why:** established CPD and training providers lack AI-on-real-work content.
**Candidates:** CPD providers for property professionals; business schools and polytechnics; Nigerian training institutes and incubators [verify].
**Offer:** white-label or co-delivered practice modules; a facilitator licence later.
**Risk:** channel conflict; brand dilution. Start with co-delivery, not white-label.

### 2.4 Franchise groups and buying groups
**Why:** one introduction can reach many branches.
**Candidates:** UK franchise and network groups, independent agent networks; Nigerian property networks [verify].
**Offer:** a group pilot with shared rule and house briefs; a per-branch rate.
**Risk:** they may mandate tools; contracting is slower. Start with one willing branch.

### 2.5 Other introducers
Accountants, solicitors, insurers and consultants who serve agencies. They want to be helpful to clients, not to sell. Offer a short brief and a free assessment for their clients. Respect conflicts of interest.

## 4. What we ask partners

- A named contact.
- A channel to reach members/customers (newsletter slot, event slot, introduction).
- Honest feedback.
- Permission before any logo use or naming. We do not use logos without written consent.

## 5. One-page partner brief (shareable)

> **Fieldwork: practical AI training for estate and letting teams**
> Fieldwork is a new training company teaching staff to use AI tools on real work: listings, enquiry replies, reports and procedures. The method has four moves: brief, run, check, keep. The principle is **AI drafts. People decide.** We never suggest AI should value a property, give legal advice or approve a transaction.
> **What exists:** a free 15-minute guided exercise; a free self-reported workflow assessment; a four-week pilot format being tested with first partners. **What does not exist:** case studies (we have none yet), a software platform (planned, not for sale).
> **What we offer your members/customers:** a 45-minute clinic; an assessment link; pilots priced after a conversation, in GBP (UK) or NGN (Nigeria).
> **What we ask:** an introduction to your audience and honest feedback.
> **What we do not do:** give legal, regulatory or valuation advice; guarantee savings or compliance.

## 7. Approach sequence

1. Pick five partners (two associations, one software user group, one training body, one franchise/network).
2. Warm introduction where possible; otherwise a short email: "We are new, with no case studies. We offer a free 45-minute clinic for your members. May I send an outline?"
3. Meet, share the brief, agree a trial event, no money first.
4. After the event, review attendance and leads; decide on referral terms.
5. Log in CRM as accounts of type "Partner".

## 8. Qualification of partners (score 0 to 2 each; 8+ to proceed)

Reach into ICP; trust with members; willingness to run a trial event; no exclusivity demand; no endorsement claim required; responsive; regulatory comfort; fit for UK or Nigeria.
