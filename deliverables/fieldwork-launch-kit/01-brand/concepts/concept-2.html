01Idea and what it borrows

A brief is the oldest tool for getting work done. Say the problem, the practice, the result.

This direction is the calm one. It takes the restraint of enterprise-grade editorial sites and applies it to a practical learning brand: off-white, black, one blue, hairline rules, tiny mono labels, one huge wordmark, and the generated field rendered as halftone terrain. Every exercise page and every sales page is built from the same triple: Problem, Practice, Result.

It is the strongest register for the business and real estate side, and it keeps the free exercise quiet and focused. Its risk is that, without colour, it can feel cold and could be mistaken for a consultancy.

Paper 70%, Ink 20%, Wash 6%, Cobalt under 4%. Cobalt appears as the dot, the terrain, links and one result panel per page.

Contrast

Ink on Paper 17.5:1. Cobalt on Paper 6.9:1. Paper on Cobalt 6.9:1. Grey on Paper 4.9:1. Hairlines are decorative only.

Learning grammar

Ink instruction. Cobalt the result. Grey the aside. Colour is withheld, so each use carries weight.

11Strengths, weaknesses, risks

Strengths

  • The most credible with real estate owners and operations managers.
  • Fast, light and very accessible: one font family, almost no colour, little imagery.
  • Problem / Practice / Result gives sales pages a clear logic.
  • The dotted i is a distinctive, tiny wordmark detail.

Weaknesses

  • Cooler and more austere. Weaker for casual individual learners.
  • Little colour means the learning grammar is carried by labels, not by hue.
  • Easy to confuse with a consultancy or an AI firm without more warmth.

Risks

  • Anveril-like restraint can look imitative if corner letters and dots are overused.
  • The i-dot disc is fine detail that needs care at 16px.
  • Not tested with users.
