# Follow-up templates

Version 0.1, 14 October 2026. Plain, honest messages for each stage. Replace [PLACEHOLDERS]. Send within the stated time. Never invent results or clients. British English. Adapt tone for WhatsApp in Nigeria (shorter, warmer, same content).

Review meeting: [DATE/TIME]. Please bring your view on keep, change or stop.

Two requests, entirely optional: honest written feedback, and whether you'd be willing to be described (anonymously or by name; you approve the exact text). No pressure either way.

[YOUR NAME]

**After the review:** send final invoice; record outcome, measures, feedback and permission status in CRM; update SALES-PRICING-ASSUMPTIONS.md with actual hours.

## Rules for all follow-up
- Send on the promised day.
- One ask per message.
- Never describe a pilot outcome as a proven result.
- If you cite a number, say where it came from.
- Do not name a client publicly without their written permission to the exact text.
