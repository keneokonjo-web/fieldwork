01The idea

Quiet, precise, at home.

Halden is a fictional international real-estate agency used to show how a Fieldwork pack works. Everything below (the logo, colours, type, posts and ads) is invented for the demonstration. For a real client, each page is built around their own name, markets and homes.

CalmLots of space, one idea per post, never shouting. Price and place are the headline.
HonestPlain words, true facts, no invented numbers. If we do not know it, we do not say it.
InternationalNot tied to one city. The same look works for London, New York and Lagos listings.
VoiceShort sentences. One italic word for warmth. “Homes worth coming home to.”
02Logo

A letter and a full stop.

The wordmark is lowercase, tightly spaced, with a red full stop. The “h.” mark stands alone as the profile picture, the favicon and the app icon.

08Captions and prompts

So your team can keep going.

Example caption · property spotlight

Marlow Lodge, Richmond. Four bedrooms, a long south-facing garden and five minutes from the river. £2,395,000. Book a viewing: link in bio.

Example caption · lettings

To let: a two-bedroom garden flat with its own entrance, close to the canal. £3,100 pcm. Available now.

Property spotlight prompt

Write a short Instagram caption for [address]. Use only these verified facts: [paste facts]. Warm, plain, no exclamation marks, under 60 words. End with “Book a viewing: link in bio”.

New listing prompt

Draft a LinkedIn post announcing [property] now on the market. Mention the area, the price and two features from the fact sheet only. Do not invent details. Add a one-line call to action.

Market note prompt

Turn these three bullet points into a 120-word market note in our house voice: calm, honest, specific. Do not state figures that I have not supplied.
