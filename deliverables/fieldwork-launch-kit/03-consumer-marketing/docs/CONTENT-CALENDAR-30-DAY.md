# 30-day content calendar

Version 0.1, prepared 14 October 2026. Day 1 is assumed to be Monday 19 October 2026 (ASSUMPTION: change the start date and the weekdays shift; the sequence stays the same).

Read `CONSUMER-MARKETING-STRATEGY.md` first for pillars and CTA rules, and `VIDEO-PRODUCTION-KIT.md` for scripts V1 to V10. Every number that appears in this calendar is a plan, not a result. Status starts at `Not started` for every item; update it in `CONTENT-TRACKER.csv`.

## How to use this calendar

- Roughly 4 hours of production on one batch day (Sunday or Saturday), 15 to 20 minutes a day for posting and replies. See `CONTENT-PRODUCTION-WORKFLOW.md`.
- One video is shot once and posted to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Upload natively to each; do not post a watermarked copy of another app's export (verify each platform's current guidance).
- Every link carries UTMs per `FUNNEL-AND-METRICS.md`. The `utm_content` value is the item id in the last column of each day.
- Four rest days (7, 14, 21, 28) are used for review, not posting. Skipping a post day is allowed; skipping a review is not.
- Items marked ONLY if... depend on a tool or evidence that may not exist. Skip them rather than fake them.
- Every fact in a script is drawn from the live site: one free exercise (brief, run, check, keep; about 15 minutes; no account; entries stay on the device) and four Field Notes. Never imply guided projects or membership can be bought.

## Notes on repurposing

- Source of truth for each video is the script in `VIDEO-PRODUCTION-KIT.md`. Captions come from the caption templates there and in `SOCIAL-KIT.md`.
- A text idea becomes a carousel, a carousel becomes Stories, a video becomes a text post. Do not re-edit; re-title.
- If a day's post fails (illness, power, data), move it, do not double up. Power and data problems are normal; batch and schedule where a platform allows it (verify before use).

## After day 30

Use the week-4 review to decide the next 30 days. Keep the two best pillars, retire the weakest format, and keep running the honest status post monthly.
