# Content production workflow

Version 0.1, 14 October 2026. A repeatable weekly loop from idea to published post, using AI the way Fieldwork teaches it: brief, run, check, keep. One person, about 6 to 8 hours a week (ASSUMPTION).

**Rule zero:** AI drafts. A person decides. Nothing is published that you have not read in full and checked against the live site and your own notes.

## 1. The weekly loop

| Step | When | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Review | Mon | 30 min | Filled `WEEKLY-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.csv`; one change decided |
| 2. Idea | Mon | 30 min | 5 to 8 candidate ideas from comments, questions, Field Notes |
| 3. Select | Mon | 15 min | Next week's items assigned to calendar days (`CONTENT-TRACKER.csv`) |
| 4. Script | Tue | 60 min | Scripts/captions drafted, fact-checked |
| 5. Produce | Batch day | 3 to 4 hours | Video shot, edited, captioned; graphics made |
| 6. QA | Batch day | 30 min | Pre-publish checklist passed |
| 7. Publish / schedule | Daily | 15 to 20 min | Posts live with UTM links |
| 8. Reply | Daily | 15 min | Comments/DMs answered |
| 9. Log | Friday | 15 min | Tracker updated with URLs and numbers |

Keep a running "claims register" (a simple list): each factual statement you make about Fieldwork, with the page that proves it. Update when the product changes.

## 2. Sources of ideas

1. Comments and DMs: what people ask, what they got wrong.
2. Starter tasks in the exercise (weekly update, customer reply, study notes, property listing).
3. Field Notes: each article produces 3 to 5 posts.
4. Interview notes (see pricing doc).
5. Your own real tasks (fictionalised).
Never invent a story about a person. If you use a real example, get permission and remove identifying details.

## 3. The Fieldwork discipline for AI-assisted content

Use the five-part brief, then the six checks.

**Brief:** Task, Audience, Tone, Shape, Rules (always include: "Do not invent facts, numbers, testimonials, or tool capabilities. Mark anything uncertain as [CHECK].").
**Run:** in any AI tool you can use (free versions fine; limits change).
**Check:** facts vs source, nothing invented, nothing missing, sounds like us, format, safe to share.
**Keep:** save the working prompt in a prompt library (a document), with the date and what you changed.

**Do not paste into AI tools:** subscriber emails, comments with names, private interview notes, passwords, anything not public. Use placeholders. See the Field Note "What not to paste".

## 4. Reusable prompt templates

Replace bracketed text. Paste your own material under MATERIAL. Keep the Rules block.

### 4.1 Idea generation

```
TASK: Suggest 10 short-video or post ideas for Fieldwork, a practical AI learning company for individuals in the UK and Nigeria.
AUDIENCE: Non-technical learners, students and early-career workers, who use free AI tools and may have limited data.
TONE: Plain, practical, honest. British English. No hype words (revolutionise, unlock, supercharge). No fear of missing out.
SHAPE: A table with columns: idea, pillar (Do it / Check it / Tool honesty and safety / Field Notes / Behind the build / Community), the task shown, the check shown, a 7-word hook, and the one CTA. Keep each cell to one line.
RULES:
- Only use these facts about Fieldwork: one free guided exercise (brief, run, check, keep; about 15 minutes; no account; entries stay on the device); four Field Notes (five-part brief, checking AI answers, five property tasks, what not to paste).
- Guided projects and membership are planned and NOT on sale. Never imply they can be bought.
- Do not invent statistics, testimonials, customers or results.
- Do not suggest anything that needs real personal data.
MATERIAL: [Paste this week's top 5 comments or questions with names removed, and the list of pillars you are short on.]
```

**Check:** Does each idea use only the allowed facts? Remove any idea implying a live product. Pick 3.

### 4.2 Script drafting

```
TASK: Draft a [30/45/60]-second vertical video script about: [topic].
AUDIENCE: [UK students / Nigerian small-business sellers / etc.]; they are watching on a phone, often without sound.
TONE: A friendly colleague who tried it first. Plain words. British English.
SHAPE: Table with columns: time, shot, voiceover (spoken words only), on-screen text (max 8 words). Start with a hook in 3 seconds. End with exactly one CTA: [CTA].
RULES:
- Use only this material and fictional examples; do not add facts.
- Say "illustrative" where the example is fictional.
- Include a visible check step.
- No guarantees, no hype, no urgency.
- Mark anything you are unsure about as [CHECK].
MATERIAL: [Paste the relevant section of the Field Note or the exercise task, copied from the live site.]
```

**Check:** Read aloud; time it; verify every claim against the claims register.

### 4.3 Fact-checking

```
TASK: Fact-check the draft below against my source material.
AUDIENCE: Me, the founder, before I publish.
TONE: Direct. No reassurance.
SHAPE: Three lists. (1) Each claim in the draft with the exact line in MY SOURCE that supports it. (2) Claims with no support in MY SOURCE. (3) Anything in MY SOURCE that the draft contradicts or omits.
RULES:
- Use only MY SOURCE. Do not use your general knowledge to approve a claim.
- If a claim is about a third-party tool, platform feature or law, list it under "verify before use" instead.
- Quote the source lines exactly.
DRAFT: [paste draft]
MY SOURCE: [paste facts copied from the site and notes]
```

**Check:** Spot-check three quotes against the source yourself. The tool can be wrong. Verify platform and legal points on the official page.

### 4.4 Caption writing

```
TASK: Write 3 caption options for [platform] for the post below.
AUDIENCE: [segment].
TONE: Plain, calm, British English. One idea only.
SHAPE: Each caption under [N] characters (verify the platform limit), first line is the hook, last line is the CTA with [LINK] as a placeholder. 3 hashtag suggestions per option (I will verify them).
RULES:
- No hype words. No claims about results. No promise that anything unavailable can be bought.
- Keep any numbers exactly as in the script.
- Include "illustrative example" if the script says so.
POST SCRIPT: [paste]
```

**Check:** character counts, tone, hashtag meaning, link, alt text.

### 4.5 Repurposing

```
TASK: Turn the video script below into (a) a LinkedIn text post, (b) a 7-slide carousel outline, (c) a WhatsApp Status text of under 40 words, and (d) a 4-line email teaser.
AUDIENCE: [segment per format].
TONE: Plain, British English.
SHAPE: Label each output clearly. For the carousel give slide text only (max 18 words per slide).
RULES:
- Keep every fact exactly as in the script.
- Do not add new claims, statistics or examples.
- Keep one CTA per output: [CTA].
- Mark anything that needs checking as [CHECK].
SCRIPT: [paste]
```

**Check:** compare each output with the script, line by line, for added claims.

### 4.6 Comment reply drafts (optional)

```
TASK: Draft a short, kind reply to this comment (names removed).
RULES: Do not invent facts about Fieldwork. If the answer depends on something not available, say it is planned and not on sale. British English. Under 40 words.
COMMENT: [paste, anonymised]
```

## 5. Pre-publish checklist (every post)

**Truth**
- [ ] Every claim about Fieldwork matches the live site today (live vs planned).
- [ ] No invented numbers, results, testimonials, followers.
- [ ] Fictional/illustrative examples labelled.
- [ ] Third-party tool or platform facts verified or marked "verify before use" and removed from the post if unverified.
- [ ] No personal data or real customer data shown.

**Voice**
- [ ] British English (practise, organise, colour).
- [ ] No hype or FOMO words; no guarantees.
- [ ] Hook names a task or mistake.
- [ ] One CTA, and it matches what exists.

**Craft**
- [ ] Captions checked word by word.
- [ ] Brand colours/fonts (see `VIDEO-PRODUCTION-KIT.md`).
- [ ] Alt text on images.
- [ ] UTM link tested; item id matches tracker.

**Safety**
- [ ] No advice presented as legal, medical, financial or property-valuation advice.
- [ ] No encouragement of cheating or breaching an institution's rules.
- [ ] Music/images are licensed.

## 6. Roles and tools

| Job | Tool (free or low cost; verify terms) |
|---|---|
| Notes and tracker | A spreadsheet that opens `CONTENT-TRACKER.csv` (Google Sheets, LibreOffice, Excel) |
| Scripts | Any text editor; AI tool for drafts |
| Video | Phone + CapCut or similar free editor |
| Graphics | Canva free / Figma free |
| Link tracking | UTM links; Plausible (configured on the site) |
| Scheduling | Native platform scheduling where available |
| Email | One of Buttondown, beehiiv, Brevo, MailerLite (to be evaluated) |

## 7. Batching and resilience (power, data, time)

- Batch-shoot on one day; keep a backlog of 3 finished posts.
- Record offline; upload when connection is good. Keep exports under platform size limits.
- If you miss a day, move the post. Do not double post.
- If a week is lost, cut to the minimum: 2 videos, 1 LinkedIn post, replies.
- Keep a short "evergreen" folder: V2, V3, V4, V7 can be re-posted after 8 to 12 weeks with new hooks.

## 8. Quality log

Each month, review: posts that needed a correction, claims that turned out wrong, tools used, AI prompt versions that worked. Save the best prompts in a prompt library with date and notes. The library is a Fieldwork-style workflow card for your own marketing.

## 9. Ownership and records

- Keep sources and licences for every image/track used.
- Keep the claims register and corrections log in the same folder as the tracker.
- Delete interview recordings when no longer needed, and say how long you keep them.
