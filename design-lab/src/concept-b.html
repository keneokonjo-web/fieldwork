The Plan

Work drawn as a set of architectural sheets: precise line, dimensions, revisions and one red correction.

01Rationale

A plan is how you think before you build, and a revision is how you improve it. Fieldwork is about drafting, checking and keeping, so it borrows the drawing set.

Linework in drafting blue on warm-white paper. Mono dimension labels. Title blocks that carry sheet number, scale and revision. A single redline that marks what to check, borrowed from the way contracts and drawings are reviewed. A big literary serif for headlines keeps it human. It speaks naturally to real estate, where drawings, floor plans and specifications are everyday objects, and it makes workflows legible as diagrams you can actually follow.

The learning grammar

Blue line: the plan. Instructions and structure.

Redline: the check. What to verify or fix.

Ink: the issued result. Revision B, approved.

05Wordmark and symbol

Fieldwork

A serif wordmark with a drawn dimension line beneath it, red, with end ticks, as if the word had been measured. It says: we measure what we make. At small sizes the dimension line is dropped. The standalone symbol is a section marker: a circle with half cut away and a leader arrow, meaning "cut through a piece of real work and look inside". It is simple enough for a favicon and avatar.

10Strengths, weaknesses, risks

Strengths

  • Highest credibility with property audiences; proposals feel like professional documents.
  • Workflows become legible diagrams, which helps selling and learning.
  • Calm, precise, elegant. Easiest direction to keep consistent.
  • The redline is a strong, meaningful device for checking.

Weaknesses

  • Cooler and less playful, so weaker for casual individual learners.
  • Line drawing needs craft. Poorly drawn diagrams look amateur.
  • Less colour, so weaker for social content at a glance.

Risks

  • Could feel like an architecture practice rather than a learning brand.
  • Pastiche risk if the drafting conventions are used loosely.
  • Not yet checked against the real references.
