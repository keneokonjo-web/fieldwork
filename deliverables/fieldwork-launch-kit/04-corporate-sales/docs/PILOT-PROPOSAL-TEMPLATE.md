## 11. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Likelihood / impact (our view) | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Staff paste personal data into a tool | Medium / High | AI-use rule in Week 1; anonymised practice only; owner reinforces. |
| Tool output contains invented facts | High / Medium | Defined check step; log every error; never publish unchecked. |
| Staff do not find the time | Medium / Medium | Short sessions; named owner; scope reduced rather than slipped. |
| Results are small or inconclusive | Medium / Low | Honest review; decide to stop. |
| Free tool changes or becomes unavailable | Medium / Low | Practice is tool-agnostic; brief and check steps transfer. |
| Fieldwork is new and delivery has not been tested at scale | Known | Small cohort; weekly contact; stop clause; fee proportional. |
| Staff worry about their jobs | Medium / Medium | Be clear in the kick-off about purpose; owner communicates. Fieldwork does not recommend headcount decisions. |

## 12. What we do not claim

We do not guarantee productivity, time saved, cost saved, quality improvement or regulatory compliance. We do not give legal, valuation or financial advice. AI drafts. People decide.
