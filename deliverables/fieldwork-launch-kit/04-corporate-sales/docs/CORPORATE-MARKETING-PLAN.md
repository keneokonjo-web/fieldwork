# Corporate marketing plan: first 90 days

Version 0.1, 14 October 2026. Plan for generating conversations with estate and letting agencies in the UK and Nigeria. **All targets are hypotheses to test, not forecasts.** Resources assumed: one founder, roughly [PLACEHOLDER: 8 to 10] hours a week on marketing, modest or no paid budget. Rule: no fabricated proof. Until real evidence exists, our marketing proof is the free exercise, the assessment, the method and honesty.

## 1. Objective and what success looks like

**Objective (day 90):** at least [10] qualified conversations with agency decision-makers and [2] paid pilots signed or in proposal, learning enough to refine the offer.

**Constraints:** site is noindex until a domain and the indexing flag are set (`src/config/site.ts` `allowIndexing`). Until then, drive traffic by direct links, not search. Confirm domain and trademark position first (docs/operations/DOMAIN-AND-TRADEMARK.md). Business contact email and legal entity details must be filled via environment variables before outreach.

## 2. Message house

- **Core line:** Practise AI on real work.
- **Business promise:** Your team, practising on your own work.
- **Real estate line:** Better listings, faster replies, clearer procedures. With people still in charge.
- **Safety line:** AI drafts. People decide.
- **Honesty line:** Fieldwork is new. We would rather show you a worked scenario than invent a testimonial.
- **Never say:** unlock, leverage, supercharge, revolutionise; "proven"; "guaranteed"; any invented statistic.

## 4. The assessment as lead magnet

- Position: "Five minutes. Eight questions. Where would AI practice help most at your agency? Self-reported, not an audit."
- Place on: LinkedIn posts, email signature, outreach emails 2 and 3, partner newsletters, the event close.
- **Flow:** assessment, report (level, three priority workflows, constraints), prospect optionally sends the summary, founder replies within one working day with one specific observation and an offer of a 20-minute call.
- **Consent:** only contact people who ask to be contacted. Do not email people just because they used the tool. [Founder to confirm what data the tool stores or sends; align privacy notice.]
- Measure: starts, completions, summaries sent to us, calls booked (see section 9).

## 5. Content plan (weekly rhythm)

**Pillars:** (1) Checking beats trusting. (2) Rules for staff. (3) Listing quality. (4) Procedures and new starters. (5) The honest startup log.

**Weekly output (minimum):** 2 LinkedIn posts, 1 Field Notes article or a short business note, 1 outreach batch.

| Week | LinkedIn post themes | Field Notes / long form |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why we say "AI drafts. People decide." / What we will never suggest AI does | "Seven everyday tasks, and what people keep" |
| 2 | The fictional listing: three details a tool might add | "The check step: how to compare a draft with a fact sheet" |
| 3 | A one-page AI-use rule for a small agency (outline) | "What to put in a staff AI-use rule (not legal advice)" |
| 4 | Behind the scenes: building the pilot | "Fieldwork is new: what exists and what doesn't" |
| 5 | Enquiry replies: two tones, one office standard | "Enquiry replies without sounding like a robot" |
| 6 | Viewing feedback: don't soften the bad news | "Summaries that quietly drop the negatives" |
| 7 | Procedures to checklists: what changed in the step | "A tidy checklist that quietly changes a step" |
| 8 | Nigeria: practice on free tiers and mobile data | "AI practice that works on a phone" |
| 9 | Question of the week from discovery calls (anonymised) | "Ten questions agency owners ask about AI" |
| 10 | Assessment: what each level means | "Foundation, emerging, ready: what to do next" |
| 11 | Founder log: what I learned from [N] calls | Case-style note **only from real conversations** |
| 12 | Pilot invitation: two founding-partner places | "How a four-week pilot works" |

Post formats: text with a concrete example, a screenshot of the fact-check table, a short carousel. No stock-photo hype. Always label examples as fictional.

## 6. LinkedIn plan

- **Profile:** founder headline "Practical AI training for property teams. Practise AI on real work." About section stating Fieldwork is new. Featured: assessment and free exercise.
- **Company page:** create when ready; link to site.
- **Routine (30 minutes a day):** 10 minutes commenting thoughtfully on posts by agency owners and property bodies, 10 minutes sending 2 personalised connection notes (OUTREACH-KIT.md), 10 minutes replying.
- **Posting:** Tuesday and Thursday. Hypothesis: owners read early in the week; test.
- **Targets (hypotheses):** 150 relevant connections in 90 days; 4 inbound conversations. Track in CRM.
- No automation, no mass tagging, no fake engagement.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Name overlap with market-research "fieldwork" firms | Trademark advice; use "Fieldwork for Business" and the tagline; check search results |
| No proof | Be explicit about being new; use fictional examples labelled as such; earn proof through pilots |
| Over-claiming | Review every post against the honesty rules |
| Data protection in outreach | Section 11 of OUTREACH-KIT.md; verify with ICO guidance/solicitor |
| Founder capacity | Cap pilots at two concurrently |
| Regulatory statements | Never state rules as fact; mark verify; refer to professional bodies |

## 11. Review decisions at day 90

Continue, change segment, change offer or pause. Criteria (hypotheses): at least 2 pilots signed AND feedback suggests clear value; or at least 8 calls with a consistent unmet need. If neither, change the offer before spending more on marketing.
