# Sales operating rhythm

Version 0.1, 14 October 2026. A weekly cadence for a founder selling alone. All time allocations and targets are assumptions to adjust. Principle: small, consistent actions, honest records, and learning faster than you spend.

## 1. Weekly shape (about 12 to 14 hours of selling work)

| Day | Block | Hours | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Plan and review | 1.5 | Review last week's numbers; set three goals; clear tracker (CRM-FIELDS-AND-PIPELINE.md) |
| Monday | Outreach | 1.5 | Send 10 personalised first touches (OUTREACH-KIT.md) |
| Tuesday | Content | 1.5 | Write and schedule two LinkedIn posts (CORPORATE-MARKETING-PLAN.md) |
| Tuesday | Calls and demos | 2 | Discovery and demo slots (DISCOVERY-CALL-GUIDE.md, DEMO-SCRIPT.md) |
| Wednesday | Follow-ups | 1 | Cadence step 2 and 3; proposal chases |
| Wednesday | Proposals | 1.5 | Write and send proposals (PILOT-PROPOSAL-TEMPLATE.md) |
| Thursday | Calls and demos | 2 | Second slot |
| Thursday | Partners | 1 | Partner outreach and relationship notes (PARTNERSHIP-PACK.md) |
| Friday | Admin and tracker | 1 | Update CRM; send invoices; log hours |
| Friday | Learn | 1 | Read objection log; update scripts |

When pilots are live, protect delivery time first. Never schedule more than two concurrent pilots.

## 2. Daily (15 minutes)
- Check replies and assessment summaries; respond within SLA.
- Update stage and next action on every touched deal.
- Send follow-ups due today.

## 3. Weekly numbers (metrics; hypotheses for targets)

| Metric | Weekly target (hypothesis) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New outreach touches | 10 per market | Quality over volume |
| Replies | 1 to 2 | Reply rate tracked, unknown |
| Discovery calls held | 1 to 2 | |
| Demos held | 1 | |
| Proposals sent | 0 to 1 | |
| New LinkedIn connections (relevant) | 10 | |
| Assessment completions | 3 to 5 | |
| Partner conversations | 1 | |
| Delivery hours logged (pilots) | actual | feed pricing |

Lagging metrics (monthly): pilots signed, quoted value, win rate, cycle length (days from first reply to signature), average discount, objections ranked.

## 4. Weekly review (30 minutes, Monday)

Answer in writing:
1. What happened? (numbers against targets)
2. Which deals moved stage? Which are stuck more than 14 days?
3. Which objections were new or frequent? Which responses worked?
4. Which sources and messages produced replies?
5. What did I promise and not do?
6. What is the one change for this week?
7. Is anything in my materials now untrue or out of date (price, claim, status)?

Record the answers in a running log with the date.

## 5. Monthly review (60 minutes, first Monday)

- Pipeline by stage; conversion between stages; average days in stage.
- Pricing: check actual hours against SALES-PRICING-ASSUMPTIONS.md; update assumptions.
- ICP: who is actually responding? Adjust tier definitions and lead scoring.
- Marketing: which content produced conversations? Stop one thing, start one thing.
- Partnerships: status of each; next ask.
- Legal and compliance: opt-outs honoured; suppression list updated; privacy notice still accurate; any claims that have become untrue.
- Capacity: hours used; sustainable?
- Decision: continue, adjust offer, adjust segment.

## 6. Quarterly decision point (day 90)

Use the criteria in CORPORATE-MARKETING-PLAN.md section 11. Also ask: is the real-estate focus right? Recruitment and other sectors come later and only after real-estate delivery evidence exists.

## 7. Honesty checklist (before sending anything)

- Does it claim a client, result, number or partnership that does not exist? Remove.
- Is every price labelled a proposal in local currency, excluding tax?
- Does it promise time saved, compliance or cost savings? Remove.
- Does it imply the onboarding platform exists or is for sale? Remove.
- Have I said Fieldwork is new, where relevant?
- Is any regulatory statement marked "verify" or removed?
- Is the example labelled fictional?

## 8. Tools (keep it small)
Spreadsheet CRM (CRM-TEMPLATE.csv), calendar, LinkedIn, email, a notes file, a time log. Add tools only when the lack of them costs you deals.

## 9. Warning signs and responses

| Sign | Response |
|---|---|
| Many calls, no proposals | Tighten qualification; check offer clarity |
| Many proposals, no signatures | Review objections; test a smaller scope; check price reaction |
| Replies only from Nigeria or only UK | Shift effort, but keep separate pricing and messaging |
| Always discounting | Review value story; keep discounts tied to feedback |
| Delivery hours over plan by more than 25% | Re-scope or reprice; build templates |
| Burnout | Cut to one market for 30 days |
