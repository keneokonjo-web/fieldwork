## 3. Storyboards for the videos (9:16, timings approximate)

**Video 1: What Fieldwork is (about 25 s)**
0 to 2.5 s big kinetic type "Every enquiry. Every viewing." over halftone dots. 2.5 to 5 notifications stack on a phone. 5 to 8 the wordmark builds. 8 to 12 a property website slides in with listing cards. 12 to 15 enquiry then reply bubbles. 15 to 19 calendar confirm and a reminder. 19 to 23 three chips: Fixed price / Your own accounts / Beside your CRM. 23 to 25 end card with the logo and call to action.

**Video 2: Websites and listings (about 26 s)**
Add-listing form fills (photos, price), a Publish button, the property card appears on the website with a "For sale" badge, search chips filter the grid, the badge flips to Reserved then Sold, a viewing request button pulses, end card.

**Video 3: Automations (about 27 s)**
Enquiry row lands, instant reply on a phone, agent notification, one-click confirm, confirmation and next-day reminder, feedback card arrives with a follow-up prompt, "Nothing slips. Nothing waits.", end card.

## 4. ElevenLabs scripts: paste each block once and generate one file per video

The pauses use `<break time="..." />` tags (Multilingual v2). If the model reads them out or ignores them, remove the tags and keep the line breaks.
