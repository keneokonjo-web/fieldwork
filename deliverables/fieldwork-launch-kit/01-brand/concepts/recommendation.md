# Concept decision (final)

The founder reviewed the round-2 concepts and chose **Concept 2, The Brief**, for the whole brand and website (round 4). An earlier blend of concepts 2 and 3 was built and then replaced; Concept 1 was rejected; Concept 3 is not used.

What carries through from Concept 2: the off-white, black and one-blue palette; Schibsted Grotesk and Geist Mono; hairlines; corner marks; the halftone dot terrain; the giant cropped wordmark with the halftone dot on the i; bracket numerals; index lists; Problem / Practice / Result. The survey-target icon from Concept 3 is kept as the app icon, favicon and avatar.

Built in the repository: logo system (`scripts/brand/build-identity.mjs`), terrain art (`build-art.mjs`), tokens (`src/styles/tokens.css`), website, templates (`brand/templates`) and guidelines (`brand/guidelines`). The three concept boards in this folder are kept as a record.
