## Practise AI on real work.
### Practical AI training for estate agencies, letting agencies and property companies in the UK and Nigeria.

**The problem we expect to hear.** Staff already use AI tools, with no shared rules. Listings read differently from one negotiator to the next. New starters take a long time to become independent. We will check which of these is true for you in a first conversation, not assume it.

**What we do.** Your team practises on the work it already does, using four moves: **brief, run, check, keep.** They finish with reusable workflow cards and a written AI-use rule they own.

> **AI drafts. People decide.**
> We never suggest AI should value a property, give legal advice or approve an offer, tenancy or transaction.

**Workflows to start with**
1. Listings from verified facts
2. Enquiry replies and follow-ups
3. Viewing feedback and vendor or landlord updates
4. Weekly reports and internal notes
5. Procedures turned into checklists for new starters

(Also available: research, marketing posts.)
