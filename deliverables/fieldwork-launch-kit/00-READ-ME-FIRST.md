# Fieldwork launch kit

Start with FINAL-REPORT.md (what is done, tested, prepared but unconnected, and deferred).

- 01-brand: logos, favicons, guidelines PDF, templates, graphics, field art, photography with credits, tokens, brand strategy, research and concepts
- 02-website: website notes (source is the repository)
- 03-consumer-marketing: strategy, calendar, video and social kits, emails, pricing assumptions, trackers
- 04-corporate-sales: capability PDF, one-pager, deck (PPTX), discovery, demo, proposal and SOW templates, outreach, objections, CRM templates
- 05-product-and-operations: PRD (future platform, not built), architecture, SEO, deployment, domain/DNS/email/social setup, legal review list, launch checklist, limitations
- 06-qa: test results, screenshots, design review, final QA report
- 07-reusable-prompts: the reference-led design prompt

Nothing has been deployed or purchased. Every price and target is a hypothesis.
